# Part B Q&A Preparation

## Questions to Expect
- Why is this target in scope?
- Why is this finding valid?
- How did you reproduce it safely?
- Why is it high impact?
- Why did you choose this finding instead of your other candidates?
- Is it a zero-day or likely known issue?
- What evidence do you have?

## Answering Rule
Every answer should be supported by one of:
- a scope screenshot or policy note
- a reproduction step
- a screenshot / capture / artefact
- a severity argument
- a novelty / prior-art check note
