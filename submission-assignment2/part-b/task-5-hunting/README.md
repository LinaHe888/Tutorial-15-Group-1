# Task 5 - In-the-Wild Vulnerability Hunting

## Goal
Find and defend one highest-impact valid vulnerability on a legal, in-scope target.

## Required Standard
The final chosen finding must be:
- legal to test
- clearly in scope
- valid and reproducible
- strong enough to defend in presentation and Q&A

## Core Questions
1. What is the exact target?
2. Why is it in scope?
3. What is the vulnerability?
4. How can it be reproduced safely and legally?
5. What is the realistic impact?
6. Why is this the team’s highest-impact valid finding?

## Current Workflow
- identify legal targets
- collect candidate findings
- triage by validity, severity, and evidence quality
- select exactly one final finding
- prepare presentation and Q&A defence
